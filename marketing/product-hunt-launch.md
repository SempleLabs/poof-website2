## Prepped answers for questions you WILL get

**"How is this different from QuickBooks + all the AI wrappers?"**
> QuickBooks gives you tools to do the work yourself; Poof does the work. And it's not a chat wrapper — the AI posts to a real double-entry ledger with account validation and reversal entries. Job costing, budgets, and forecasting are included; QuickBooks gates those behind its $115–275/mo tiers.

**"Why should I trust an AI with my books?"**
> Fair question — it's why we run a managed service on the same engine with a controller reviewing every close. That feedback loop is how the AI gets held to professional standards. You also review and approve; the AI does the volume work, you make the calls that matter. And your data exports anytime — no lock-in.

**"$79 seems like a lot vs Wave/free tools."**
> Wave is genuinely good for free invoicing. Poof is for when the bookkeeping itself is the problem — categorization, reconciliation, job costing, close. Feature-for-feature it replaces a $115/mo QuickBooks Plus sub or a $500+/mo bookkeeper. That said: 90 days free, judge it yourself.

**"Does it do taxes/payroll?"**
> No — Poof keeps your books clean and tax-ready year-round and produces the package your CPA files from. No payroll either. Honest scope: bookkeeping, done extremely well.
