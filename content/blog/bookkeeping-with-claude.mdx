**Claude is good at the thinking parts of bookkeeping and bad at being the books.** Ask it what a gross margin drop means, how to categorize a strange charge, or how to word a reminder to a customer 60 days late, and it does well. Paste in three months of bank statements and ask it to keep your books, and you get something that looks like a ledger but doesn't balance, doesn't reconcile, and isn't there next month. The setup that works is the opposite way round: keep the books in accounting software, and let Claude **read** them. With Poof, that is a free connector in Claude's directory, and it takes three steps.

This is the post for owners who already use Claude every day and wonder why it can't just do the books.

## What Claude does well

- **Explaining.** "Why is my profit up but my bank balance down?" is a question Claude answers clearly, and it will walk through receivables, inventory and loan payments without making you feel slow.
- **Suggesting categories.** Give it a charge, "ACH WINSUPPLY 0418", and it will tell you it is probably materials from a plumbing and HVAC supply house.
- **Spotting the odd one out.** Hand it a list of charges and it notices the same amount twice in two days, or a subscription you forgot.
- **Drafting.** The collection email, the note to the CPA, the explanation for a lender of why March was slow.
- **Thinking out loud about decisions.** Lease or buy the truck, raise prices or add a tech, what a slow winter does to cash.

## Where a chat falls down as the books

**It doesn't keep a ledger.** Double-entry books are a system: every entry balances, every account carries its history, and last month's closing balance is this month's opening one. A chat starts fresh, or close to it, and nothing guarantees this month's numbers agree with last month's.

**It doesn't reconcile.** Reconciliation is matching every line on the bank statement to the books, every month, and explaining the difference to the cent. That is a repeated, exact job, and an exact job is where you want software that refuses to finish until the difference is zero.

**It has no audit trail.** Who approved this entry, when, and why? A CPA, a lender, or the IRS can ask. A chat history isn't a record.

**It doesn't file anything, or remind you.** The 1099s due February 1, the January 15 estimate, the sales tax return: a chat only knows about them if you ask.

**Statements carry a lot of private data.** Account numbers, balances, and the names of everyone you pay or who pays you. Before you upload any of that to an AI chat, check your plan's data settings and your own policy.

None of this is a knock on Claude. It is the difference between an advisor and a set of books. You want both.

## The pattern that works: books in a ledger, Claude reading them

Keep the books in accounting software, where entries balance, the bank ties out, and every change has a name on it. Then connect that software to Claude so you can ask about the books in plain words, from the chat you already have open.

That gets you the explanation and the advice, on numbers that are real and current, without pasting statements into a chat.

## Connect Poof to Claude

Poof Bookkeeping is in Claude's connector directory. You need a Poof account, which is free.

1. Open [Poof Bookkeeping in Claude's directory](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/poof-bookkeeping) and press **Connect**.

## What to ask

- "How did we do last month?"
- "Who owes us money, and how late are they?"
- "What bills are due this week?"
- "Which jobs made money this quarter?"
- "Is last month closed? What's still open?"
- "What's waiting for my approval in Poof?"

Then keep going, because this is where Claude shines. "Why was the Garcia job's margin so low?" "Draft a friendly reminder to the three customers over 60 days." "If we add a tech in March, what does that do to cash by June?"

## What the connection can and can't do

| Can Claude… | Through Poof |
|---|---|
| Read the P&L, receivables, bills, jobs, the close and the approvals inbox | Yes |
| See more than your role in Poof can see | No. An office manager's Claude gets receivables and invoices, not the P&L |
| Post an entry, send an invoice, move money | No. It is read-only |
| Approve a card | No. It can give you a link to approve it in Poof |
| See customer and vendor names | No. Names are swapped for codes before anything leaves Poof, and personal details are removed |
| Keep access after you disconnect | No. Settings → Chat apps → AI assistants turns it off, and removing someone from your company ends their AI's access too |

The read-only part is deliberate. An AI that reads your books can only be wrong in what it tells you, which you can check. An AI that writes to your books can be wrong in the books themselves. In Poof, the AI that does the work, Poof Agent, proposes every write on a card with the evidence, and a person approves it.
