**Form 1099-NEC is for work; Form 1099-MISC is for almost everything else.** Pay a contractor, a sub, a freelancer, or an attorney for their services, and it goes on a 1099-NEC. Pay rent, royalties, a prize that isn't for work, medical payments, or a settlement through a lawyer, and it goes on a 1099-MISC. For payments made in 2026, most boxes on both forms start at **$2,000**, up from $600. The NEC is due **February 1, 2027**, to the IRS and the recipient. The MISC goes to the recipient by the same day and to the IRS by **March 31, 2027** if you e-file.

Most small businesses only ever file NECs. The MISC shows up when you rent a building or a machine, pay a settlement, or give away a prize. Here is how to tell them apart for 2026.

## The short version

| You paid | Form and box | 2026 threshold |
|---|---|---|
| A contractor, sub or freelancer for work | 1099-NEC, box 1a | $2,000 |
| An attorney's fees for legal services | 1099-NEC, box 1a | $2,000 |
| Rent for a building, yard or equipment | 1099-MISC, box 1 | $2,000 |
| Royalties | 1099-MISC, box 2 | $10 |
| A prize or award not for services | 1099-MISC, box 3 | $2,000 |
| A doctor, clinic or other medical provider, as a business | 1099-MISC, box 6 | $2,000 |
| Money through an attorney that isn't their fee, like a settlement | 1099-MISC, box 10 | $600 |

The test underneath every row is the same: was the payment for someone's services? If yes, it is nonemployee compensation, and it goes on the NEC. If it was for the use of property, a prize, or money passing through, it goes on the MISC.

Two older rules still apply to both forms. Only business payments count, not personal ones. And payments by credit card, debit card or a payment app like PayPal are reported by the processor on a 1099-K, not by you.

## Form 1099-NEC, box by box

The 2026 NEC has new boxes. Box 1 is now **box 1a**.

| Box | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| 1a | Nonemployee compensation: $2,000 or more for services |
| 1b, 1c | Cash tips, and the Treasury tipped-occupation code (new for 2026) |
| 1d | Overtime compensation (new for 2026) |
| 2 | A checkbox for direct sales of $5,000 or more of consumer products for resale |
| 3 | Excess golden parachute payments, moved here from the MISC |
| 4 | Federal income tax withheld, including backup withholding |
| 5 to 7 | State information |

For most businesses, box 1a and possibly box 4 are the whole form. If you backup withheld from a contractor who never sent a W-9, you file an NEC for them whatever the amount, with the withholding in box 4.

## Form 1099-MISC, box by box

| Box | What goes in it | 2026 threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rents | $2,000 |
| 2 | Royalties | $10 |
| 3 | Other income, including prizes and awards not for services | $2,000 |
| 4 | Federal income tax withheld | Any amount |
| 5 | Fishing boat proceeds | Any amount |
| 6 | Medical and health care payments | $2,000 |
| 7 | Direct sales of $5,000 or more (checkbox) | $5,000 |
| 8 | Substitute payments in lieu of dividends or interest | $10 |
| 9 | Crop insurance proceeds | $2,000 |
| 10 | Gross proceeds paid to an attorney | $600 |
| 11 | Fish purchased for resale | $600 |
| 12 | Section 409A deferrals | $2,000 |
| 13a, 13b, 14 | Cash tips, tipped-occupation code, overtime (new for 2026) | |
| 15 | Nonqualified deferred compensation | $2,000 |
| 16 to 18 | State information | |

Notice what did not move to $2,000: royalties ($10), substitute payments ($10), attorney gross proceeds ($600), and fish purchases ($600). Nonqualified deferred compensation and the state boxes moved down two numbers to make room for the new ones.

## The cases that trip people up

**Equipment with an operator.** Renting a skid steer is rent, MISC box 1. Renting a crane and its operator is two payments: prorate the bill between the machine, on the MISC, and the operator's charge, which is nonemployee compensation on the NEC.

**Parts and labor on one bill.** A sub who bills you for labor and the materials on the job gets an NEC for the whole bill, when supplying the materials was incidental to the service. Parts you buy on their own from a supply house are merchandise and aren't reported at all.

**Attorneys.** The lawyer who drafted your contracts gets an NEC for their fees. The plaintiff's lawyer you pay a $15,000 settlement to gets a MISC, box 10, for $15,000. Both are reportable even if the firm is a corporation, which is the exception to the usual rule that corporations don't get 1099s.

**Rent through a property manager.** Rent paid to a real estate agent or property manager isn't reported by you. The agent reports what it passes to the owner. Rent paid straight to the owner, if the owner isn't a corporation, is MISC box 1.

**Prizes and awards.** A $2,500 prize in a customer drawing is MISC box 3. An award to a nonemployee for their work, like a bonus to your top outside salesperson, is NEC box 1a.

**Travel reimbursements.** If a contractor's travel costs are reimbursed without the contractor accounting to you for them, the reimbursement goes in NEC box 1a with their fee.

**Medical payments.** A shop that pays a clinic for pre-employment drug tests or physicals reports it in MISC box 6 at $2,000 or more, and that includes clinics that are corporations.

## Who doesn't get either form

- **Corporations,** including LLCs taxed as C or S corporations, except for attorneys and medical providers
- **Employees,** whose pay is on the W-2
- **Anyone you paid by card or payment app,** who gets a 1099-K from the processor
- **Merchandise, freight, storage, telephone and similar items**
- **Tax-exempt organizations and government agencies**
- **Personal payments,** like the plumber at your house

The details on LLCs, partnerships, foreign contractors and the rest are in [who gets a 1099](/blog/who-gets-a-1099).

## Different forms, different due dates

| For 2026 payments | To the recipient | To the IRS |
|---|---|---|
| 1099-NEC | February 1, 2027 | February 1, 2027 |
| 1099-MISC | February 1, 2027 (February 16 with box 8 or 10) | March 1, 2027 on paper, March 31, 2027 electronically |

The usual NEC date is January 31; in 2027 it is a Sunday. The MISC's later IRS date is why getting the form right matters: an NEC filed on the MISC's schedule is two months late. If you file 10 or more information returns of all kinds in total, you must file them electronically, and from 2027 that means through IRIS or a filer that uses it.
