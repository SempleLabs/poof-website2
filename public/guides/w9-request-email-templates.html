W-9 Request Emails

Four templates for collecting Form W-9 before the 2026 1099s are due on February 1, 2027

Poof · written by a former controller

You need a W-9 from every contractor you pay, even under the new $2,000 threshold for 2026 payments. These four emails cover the first ask, a reminder, the final note, and the corporation that asks why. Replace everything in [brackets], and send each one from your own address.

Before you send

  • Use the current form, Rev. March 2024The IRS's draft June 2026 revision is not final yet. The current form is at irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf.
  • Ask at the first paymentFor new contractors, before the first check. For everyone you already pay without a W-9, this week.
  • Give them a secure way to send itA filled-in W-9 carries a full tax ID. Offer a secure upload or form link, not an unencrypted email attachment.
  • Keep each W-9 four years, encryptedLimit who can open them, and track who you've asked and who has answered.

1. The first ask

Send at the first payment, or now for contractors without a W-9 on file
Subject: W-9 needed for [Your Business] payments

Hi [first name],

Before [we send your first payment / the end of the year], we need a completed Form W-9 for our records. It's required for the 1099 we may file for payments made in 2026, which is due February 1, 2027.

Please fill it in here: [secure link]. If you'd rather use the IRS's form, it's at https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf, and you can upload it at [secure upload link]. Please don't email it back as an attachment, since it carries your tax ID.

It takes about five minutes. Thanks,
[your name]
[Your Business] · [phone]

2. Reminder

About a week later
Subject: Reminder: W-9 for [Your Business]

Hi [first name],

A quick reminder that we still need your Form W-9. Here's the link again: [secure link].

Thanks,
[your name]

3. The final note

About a week after the reminder
Subject: W-9 needed before your next payment

Hi [first name],

We still don't have a Form W-9 from you. Without a taxpayer ID on file, the law requires us to withhold 24% of future payments as backup withholding and send it to the IRS. We'd rather pay you in full.

Please fill in your W-9 here before [date of next payment]: [secure link]. If something on the form is unclear, reply and I'll help.

Thanks,
[your name]

4. For a corporation that asks why

When a vendor replies that corporations don't get 1099s
Subject: Re: W-9 for [Your Business]

Hi [first name],

You're right that payments to corporations generally aren't reported on a 1099. We still need a W-9 from you, because it's how we document that you're exempt. Your tax classification and any exempt payee code on the W-9 are what our records rely on, and without it we can't treat you as a corporation.

Here's the link: [secure link]. Thanks for your help,
[your name]
